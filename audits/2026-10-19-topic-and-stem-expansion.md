## Careful labeling
- Public NASA citizen science is **volunteer research**, not selective NASA internships or guaranteed mentorship placements.
- Program types remain accurate to their organizational structure. ACS ChemClub registration is through a **teacher adviser**, not an open student internship.
- Future dates are not inferred from past program years. 2027 Project SEED and USAAAO application windows remain unknown.
- The Space Apps 2026 competition requires parents/guardians to register and accompany minors during the event according to the official organizer's terms.
- Students should always verify organizer information before applying, because tasks, deadlines, platform age terms and local placement availability can change.
