# Expansion audit — 30 additional programs (October 18, 2026)

**Repository:** Student Opportunities NYC. **Old catalog:** 41. **New bundled catalog:** 71 unique programs.

## What was done

Compared 30 new opportunities against official organizer websites, and added them with specific **What you'll do** descriptions, eligibility, costs/compensation when published, links, attendance format, latest known deadlines, and borough only when the physical venue is identifiable. The official links in the table below are the review sources. No independent current application-portal submission test was performed.

**New physical borough tags:** Manhattan: 5; Bronx: 5; Queens: 4; Brooklyn: 10; Staten Island: 2; Online/multi-site/regionwide: 4.

Applications change between review and use. **Open** only describes organizer-published current cycle information as of review; it is not real-time guaranteed availability. **Closed** includes older cycles where a new opening has not been announced; some programs accept year-round interest but have no confirmed available placement. Exact deadline ISO fields exist only when the organizer published the cutoff.

## Review cautions

- **Columbia BRAINYAC:** Not open to any NYC student; participants must already be members of approved partner schools/programs (listed on its official page).
- **Bronx Zoo Project TRUE:** Bronx-resident sophomores or juniors are required, so it is not a citywide research internship.
- **NYU GSTEM:** Summer Research is a tuition-based program (2026 tuition $5,750); the spring Data Science course is also tuition-based (2026 $2,500). Aid is available according to NYU; no scholarship is guaranteed.
- **Wave Hill Forest Project:** The organizer listed a separate SYEP requirement for 2026, which must be checked again before applying in a future cohort.
- **Staten Island Zoo:** Teen volunteering and Junior Docent are appropriate for high schoolers; the zoo's *professional animal-care internships are restricted to undergraduates or graduate students*, and are not represented as teen positions.
- **Queens Botanical Garden:** The source explicitly welcomes student/service groups, but individual teen minimum ages are not established; this listing does **not** promise a placement.
- **BRIC:** Teen Curatorial and Teen Media are distinct fellowships, but the organizer says a student may apply to only one in the same cycle. Both 2026–27 fellowships closed in September.
- **NYC borough filtering:** On the live directory, choosing a borough excludes opportunities that have no confirmed single physical borough, such as online or regionwide events; that remains intentional.
- **Source review ≠ a working application:** Links to previous cohorts remain useful for watching the next year's process, but should not be mistaken for current openings.
