Privacy & Data

Student Opportunities NYC is an independent directory for students. This page explains the website features that use or store information.

Saved programs and notes

Without an account, your favorites and application notes are saved in your own browser and can disappear if its data is cleared. With sign-in, they are stored using Supabase so you can access them on another device. Your notes are not listed publicly; do not enter passwords, student IDs, medical details, or other sensitive information.

Accounts and student suggestions

Sign-in is provided through Supabase and supported email or Google authentication. When you suggest a program, the organizer name, program name, website, optional notes, and your account identifier are saved for administrator review and spam prevention. Suggestions are not public by default. Only an administrator can approve them and create a public listing.

Anonymous website statistics

The site counts page loads by day and page category (homepage, program pages, or student dashboard) without storing a visitor's name, account ID, IP address, full page URL, or a tracking cookie in the site's statistics table. Counts are approximate and include repeat visits. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical logs according to their own policies.

Nearby NYC youth program discovery

Visitors can choose a borough to load DYCD-funded youth program locations from the public NYC Open Data service. The selected borough is saved locally in this browser so it can be reused on later visits, and is included in requests to the city data provider. Using the optional “Use my location” button asks the browser for location permission; coordinates are used only temporarily in the browser to calculate distances and are not saved, sent to our site, or included in Open Data requests. NYC Open Data may independently record standard network access information. These automated listings are not reviewed application openings, and visitors should confirm details with the organization.

External websites and program information

Official program links lead to independent organizations with their own privacy terms. A program being listed does not mean that applications are currently open. Dates, eligibility, and status should always be confirmed directly with the organizer.

Review and corrections

Students can use the site's Report an Issue option while signed in to report mistakes. Administrators can review, update, or remove listings and inappropriate suggestions. Please ask a trusted adult for assistance if you have concerns about signing up for third-party programs or sharing personal data.
